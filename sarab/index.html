Our Story

Welcome to Ucodes
Authentic Nigerian Restaurant

Founded with a deep devotion to preserving Nigeria's rich culinary roots, Ucodes began as an authentic eatery on Ehimiri Street, Umuahia. Our passion is honoring the soul, aroma, and vibrant warmth of home-style African cooking. Today, our seasoned Nigerian chefs proudly serve thousands of happy diners every week with generational recipes that bring people together.

100% Indigenous Nigerian Ingredients

We source sun-ripened habanero peppers, fresh stockfish, fermented locust beans (iru), and palm oil directly from local Nigerian farmers daily.

Master Nigerian Chefs & Firewood Traditions

Our kitchen celebrates generational culinary craft — firewood party jollof, stone-ground yaji spices, and slow-simmered native clay pots.

Steaming-Hot Express Delivery

Order online and receive your favourite Nigerian native soups, hot swallows, and sizzling suya delivered piping fresh in under 25 minutes.
